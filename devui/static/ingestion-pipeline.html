HOW TO USE THIS
Change one setting at a time.
Start with the structure-aware baseline. Change overlap, build a saved trial, and inspect its text and PDF highlights. You can also change target length or maximum length.
Target is a grouping goal; headings and tables may produce shorter chunks. Overlap applies only at size boundaries. Moving a slider does not call Fireworks. Building a semantic trial can use embedding credits.
Saved trials are separate chunk files. Their settings remain visible here so you can evaluate them after retrieval is built.
